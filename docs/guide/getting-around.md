# Getting around

## The sidebar

The sidebar holds what you come back to:

- **Issues**: the issue list of a project, the one you viewed last.
- **My issues**: the issues assigned to you, in every project.
- Under **Workspace**: **Projects**, if you create projects or manage one, and **Settings**, if you manage part of the instance.

At its foot, your name and email lead to [My account](./account), the icon beside them signs you out, and the version of Toppo running shows at the very bottom: give it when you report a problem. Those who manage the instance also see there when a newer version is out.

On a phone or a narrow window, the sidebar hides behind the menu button at the top left (**Open navigation**) and closes once you have chosen.

## The top bar

- **The project**: on the issue list, the breadcrumb `Projects / Website ▾ / Issues` switches project; its menu searches them by name.
- **Search**: opens the command palette (below).
- **The bell**: your [notifications](./notifications), with the number unread.

## The command palette

Press **Ctrl K** (**⌘ K** on a Mac), from anywhere, or click **Search** in the top bar. Press it again, or **Esc**, to close it.

- Before you type, it lists what you can open: **New issue** (in the project you viewed last), **Issues**, **My issues**, and **Projects** and **Settings** if you may see them.
- Type words to find issues in every project you can see, typos forgiven, or a key such as `WEB-42` to open that issue: the 10 best matches show, each with its project and status. See [finding issues](./finding-issues#search).
- **↑** and **↓** move through the choices, **Enter** opens one.

## The keyboard

Toppo has one shortcut of its own, **Ctrl K** / **⌘ K**; everything else is reached with **Tab**, **Enter** and **Esc**, as on any page. The first **Tab** reaches a **Skip to content** link. A few places answer to more keys:

- In a field you type into, **Enter** saves and **Esc** puts back what was there.
- In the list of people offered after `@`, **↑ ↓** move, **Enter** or **Tab** chooses, **Esc** closes.
- In the attachment viewer, **←** and **→** move between files.

## On a phone

Every screen works from a 320-pixel-wide phone up, with a finger or with a screen reader. Below 640 pixels, the issue list shows each issue as a card, and a **Sort** menu replaces the clickable column headers.
