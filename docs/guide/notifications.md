# Notifications

Toppo tells you of what happens on the issues you follow, in the app and by email.

## Who is told of what

You follow an issue when you reported it, when it is assigned to you, when you comment on it, or when you click **Follow**. **Unfollow** stops it for good, even if you comment again.

| What happens                   | Who is told                                                   |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A new issue                    | The members of roles told of every new issue (**New issues**) |
| An issue assigned              | The new assignee, and the issue's followers                   |
| A comment                      | The issue's followers, and the people it mentions             |
| A comment edited               | Only the people it mentions for the first time                |
| A status changed               | The issue's followers                                         |
| An issue marked as a duplicate | The issue's followers                                         |

You are never told of what you did yourself, nor of an issue you may not see.

## The bell

The bell in the top bar counts what you have not read, and lists the latest 20: open one to go to its issue, which marks it read. **Mark all as read** clears the count; **See all** opens the **Notifications** page, 20 a page. Read notifications go after 90 days.

## By email

A notification you have not read within 5 minutes is sent to you by email, in your language, when [email](../self-hosting/email) is set up. In **My account › Notifications**, choose which kinds reach you by email:

- New issues (if your role tells you of them)
- Issues given to me
- Assignments of the issues I follow
- Comments on the issues I follow
- Mentions
- Status changes of the issues I follow
- Duplicates among the issues I follow

All are on to start with. In the app, you are told of all of them.
